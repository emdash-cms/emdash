The rich text editor's link input now searches existing content by title as you type, so authors can link to pages and posts without copying URLs. Results include drafts (marked as such) when the signed-in user may read them, and picking a result inserts the entry's public URL. Entries whose URL pattern needs a publish date can't be picked until they are published. Typing or pasting a URL works as before.
