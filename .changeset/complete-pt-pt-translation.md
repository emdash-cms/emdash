Completes the European Portuguese (`pt-PT`) admin translation. Portuguese admins now see localized text in the revisions panel, the bylines form, the Change domain dialog, the video block, the editor's block menu, the in-page editing toolbar, the menu and SEO settings, the domain-change emails, and the error messages shown when a request fails. Previously, 349 strings fell back to English.
