Fixes the blocks field in the content editor growing wider than its column when a block's header or a repeater item's header showed long text, such as a testimonial quote or an FAQ question. Blocks ran under the settings panel on desktop and past the edge of the screen on phones, hiding **Add block** and each block's duplicate and delete buttons. Long header text now ends in an ellipsis, and the blocks stay inside the column.
