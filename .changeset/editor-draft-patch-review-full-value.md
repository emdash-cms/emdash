Shows the whole value when you review a sandboxed plugin's proposed editor changes. "Before" and "After" previously showed only the text of a rich text field, cut at 160 characters, so an added HTML block, image or embed, a changed link, a formatting change, or an edit further down did not appear. The review now shows plain paragraphs as text and every other block in full, including headings, lists, bold or italic text and links, and shows other values in full instead of truncating them.
