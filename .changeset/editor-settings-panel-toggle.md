Adds a **Hide settings** button at the end of the content editor's bar on screens 1024px and wider. Hiding the settings panel centers the page in the full width, and **Show settings** brings the panel back. The panel starts open, and each browser remembers the choice. A hidden panel opens on its own for image and gallery settings, and when Save is blocked by an invalid field inside it. While a version is open with the panel hidden, the bar shows the version stepper.
