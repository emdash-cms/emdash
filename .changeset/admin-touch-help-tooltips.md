Fixes the info buttons beside admin field labels doing nothing when tapped on a phone or tablet. In the content editor, the content list's byline filter, and the filename, alt text and focal point fields in media details, a tap now shows the help, which stays open until the next tap, a tap elsewhere, or Escape. Hovering over or focusing an info button still shows its help, and a mouse click now keeps it open the same way.
