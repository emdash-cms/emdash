Adds a breadcrumb trail to the admin header on pages that sit under another page, such as **Settings › General**, **Menus › Main navigation**, **Content Types › Relations** and **Media › Product photos**. The parent crumb replaces the back buttons and links those pages had, including the media library's **Back** button and its in-page folder trail. Top-level pages and the content editor show no trail. Leaving a media folder by any route, including the browser's Back button, now keeps the library's scroll position and moves focus to its heading. On phones, **View Site** and the **Staging** badge in the header show only their icons.
